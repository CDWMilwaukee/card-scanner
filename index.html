⚖️ Scarcity Arbitrage — scarcer cards priced too cheap

Within the same player + year + product + auto status, a scarcer card should cost more than a looser one — but the market prices rarity on a dampened curve (≈rarity^0.65), not linearly. A /50 is 3× scarcer than a /150 but typically trades ~1.7×. These are scarcer cards priced below that parity vs a looser tier of the exact same card. Unnumbered autos treated as /800.
